## Synthesis

The founder named Warren and Factory Missions as the closest existing reference points. Their appeal is the ability to define work and step away. Vago aims to let the human use a different Workflow for each Project, including work, solo, and open source Projects, without imposing personal configuration files on collaborators. This describes the founder's desired distinction; the actual capabilities of Warren and Factory Missions have not been checked.

For a shared Project, the founder wants the choice to leave no tracked Vago files or share a `.vago` Workflow intentionally.
The founder might share a Workflow when it has proved useful for a type of work, such as bug fixing.
